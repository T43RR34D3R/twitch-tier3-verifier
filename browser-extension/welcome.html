Welcome to Twitch Chat Highlighter!

Enhance your streaming workflow with powerful chat message highlighting

🎯
Click to Highlight: Click any Twitch chat message to highlight it for your stream overlay
🔄
Real-time Sync: Highlighted messages sync with your streaming application instantly
🎨
Visual Feedback: See highlighted messages with beautiful golden styling and animations
⚙️
Customizable: Configure server settings, appearance, and behavior to fit your needs

Quick Setup:

1. Configure Server: Open the extension popup and enter your server URL and channel name
2. Visit Twitch: Go to any Twitch channel page with chat open
3. Start Highlighting: Click on chat messages to highlight them - you'll see a golden star appear!
4. Use in OBS: Your highlighted messages will appear in your streaming overlay automatically
Go to Twitch Extension Settings
